# Reader guide — thank you for lending your voice!

Your recordings will teach a computer to speak Harari. Here's all you need to know.

## How it works

1. Open your personal link (don't share it). It works on a phone or a computer.
2. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
3. Read the sentence on screen, then:
   - press **Record**, wait half a second, read the sentence, press **Stop**
   - press **Listen** if you want to check it
   - press **Redo** if you stumbled, or **Save & next** if it's good
4. Every saved sentence uploads automatically. You can stop any time — next time you open the
   link, it continues where you left off.

## For the best sound

- **Same place, every time.** A quiet room with the door closed. Curtains, carpet or a sofa help.
- **No background noise.** Turn off TV, fans, AC and notifications (put the phone on Do Not Disturb).
- **Same distance.** Hold the phone or mic about one hand-span from your mouth, slightly to the side.
- **Natural voice.** Read at your normal speed and tone — like explaining something to a friend.
- **Read exactly what's written.** If you make any mistake, press Redo.
- **Short sessions are great.** 15–30 minutes at a time. Take breaks and drink water.

## If something goes wrong

- *"Microphone not available"* — allow microphone access for the page in your browser settings and reload.
- *"Too loud"* — move back a little. *"Very quiet"* — move a little closer.
- *"Waiting to upload"* — your internet dropped. Keep the page open; it retries automatically.
- Anything else — message Mohammed.

Goal: about 2 hours of recordings in total (roughly 600–1,000 sentences). Every session helps!
