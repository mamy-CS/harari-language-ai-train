Tips for a great recording
  • Quiet room, door closed, no TV, fan or AC noise. Rooms with curtains, carpet or a sofa sound best.
  • Keep the phone or mic the same distance from your mouth every time — about one hand-span.
  • Read naturally, at your normal speed, like you are explaining something to a friend.
  • Leave a short pause after pressing Record before you speak.
  • If you stumble, press Redo. Every saved take should be read exactly as written.
  • Short sessions are fine — 15 minutes whenever you're free. Your place is saved.
